Privacy Policy

Quick Web Notepad is a simple browser-based notepad application.

Notes and content

The text you write in Quick Web Notepad is processed in your browser. The app does not require an account and does not intentionally send your notes to a server.

Browser storage

To survive an accidental reload, the text and file name of the current tab are kept in your browser's sessionStorage. This copy belongs to that tab only: a new tab starts empty, and the copy is discarded when the tab is closed. Your theme, language, zoom and word-wrap preferences are kept in localStorage. None of this leaves your device, and you can clear it from your browser settings.

Offline use and Markdown

The app stores its own files in the browser cache so it can open without a connection. The Markdown view is rendered on your device; if a note embeds an image from another website, your browser requests that image from that website.

Advertising

This site may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

You can manage personalized advertising through your Google ad settings and browser privacy settings.

Analytics

This site may use basic analytics to understand general usage patterns. Analytics tools are not intended to collect the content of your notes.
