Privacy Policy

Quick Web Notepad is a simple browser-based notepad application.

Notes and content

The text you write in Quick Web Notepad is processed in your browser. The app does not require an account and does not intentionally send your notes to a server.

Browser storage

To survive an accidental reload, the text and file name of the current tab are kept in your browser's sessionStorage, which belongs to that tab only; a new tab starts empty. So that a note can be reopened after its tab is closed, a copy of each note that is not empty is also kept in your browser's localStorage and listed under Recent. Up to 30 notes are kept, notes longer than one million characters are not, and emptying a note removes its copy. In the Recent menu you can delete one note, delete all of them, or turn this off, which deletes the kept notes. Your theme, language, zoom and word-wrap preferences are also kept in localStorage. None of this leaves your device, and you can clear it from your browser settings.

Offline use and Markdown

The app stores its own files in the browser cache so it can open without a connection. The Markdown view is rendered on your device; if a note embeds an image from another website, your browser requests that image from that website.

Advertising

This site shows advertisements through Google AdSense: on the About, Privacy and Contact pages, and in one thin strip under the editor. The app itself does not send the text you write to any server. The ad code is third-party code from Google that runs on the page.

On the pages that show ads, Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. Where the law requires it, you are asked for your consent first. You can turn off personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, and you can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Analytics

This site does not run a separate analytics tool. Google AdSense records its own ad measurements on the pages where ads appear.
